Where PM is heading
  • The generalist PM role is splitting into a "builder-PM" (ships prototypes solo with AI tools) and a "strategist/orchestrator-PM" lane — you already straddle both (Studio Firn solo shipping + Settly's multi-agent platform ownership).
  • AI fluency is now table stakes, not a differentiator: ~94% of PMs use AI daily, saving 1–2 hrs/day — the bar has moved to what you build with that time.
  • Classic hand-off boundaries between product/eng/design are dissolving into overlapping, AI-accelerated teams — a good argument for leaning into your "AI Builder" narrative in interviews.
  • Lenny's Podcast is the closest thing to a default PM podcast right now, and its interviews are increasingly AI-PM-heavy — worth a standing weekly listen.
